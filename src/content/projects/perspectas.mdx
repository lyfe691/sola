# Website for perspectas, a consulting and recruiting firm

perspectas gmbh is a Swiss firm whose two partners advise companies on staffing, recruit for them and run projects such as succession planning. I designed and built their website, [www.perspectas.ch](https://www.perspectas.ch), and set it up so they can write and publish everything on it themselves.

The site had to explain what the firm does within a few screens and make it easy to book a first conversation: every page has a «Termin buchen» (book an appointment) button in the corner, with a booking calendar for each partner. It also had to stay in their hands after launch. Adding a project or rewording a paragraph shouldn't mean writing to a developer and waiting for a reply.

## The services

The home page sets out the services as an accordion. Every title stays in view and the first one is open when you arrive, so it's clear the others hold more. Opening a service closes the one before it, and each lists what it includes, such as research, assessments or onboarding. A card under the list invites visitors to talk through their own situation.

## How they work, and the tagline

Below the services, two cards say how the firm works and how it thinks about people. Then comes the question behind the tagline on the logo, «Design your talents». The heading flashes in the logo's yellow as it scrolls into view, and the answer beside it brightens word by word as you scroll, until the whole paragraph is lit.

## Testimonials

Clients and candidates vouch for the firm in their own words. On the home page their quotes run in a slider, each cut to five lines, and «Weiterlesen» (read more) opens the full text over the page. A page of its own shows every testimonial in full.

## Projects and open positions

Projects are the part of the site that grows. Each one starts as a card with a photo and a short summary. Once the partners write the longer text, it becomes a case-study page, with the key facts in a column beside the text and a way to book a call about it.

## Design, in light and dark

The design takes one thing from the logo: the yellow dot. It's the only accent colour on the site, on an off-white page with the corporate grey for text, and it marks whatever needs marking, from the headline's full stop to the bullets in the service lists. The site follows the visitor's light or dark setting, and the button in the bottom corner switches by hand, with the new scheme opening as a circle from the button.

## Editing without a developer

The site is built with Next.js, and every text and picture on it lives in Sanity, a content editor the partners open in their browser. They edit the services, projects and testimonials there, and when they publish, the change is on the live site straight away. Anything they'd rather not show for now can be switched off instead of deleted.
